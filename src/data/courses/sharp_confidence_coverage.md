# SHARP Confidence Coverage Notes

## Justification By Faith
Justification by faith means God declares a sinner righteous through faith in Jesus Christ, not by personal merit. Key language: justification, faith, righteousness.

## Trinity
The Trinity teaches one God in three Persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. Christians use this term to summarize biblical teaching about God's triune identity.

## KJV And WEB
Difference between KJV and WEB:
- KJV means King James Version in early modern English.
- WEB means World English Bible in modern public-domain English.

## Agape In Greek
Agape is a Greek word often used for self-giving love. In Christian teaching, agape points to God's sacrificial love and the love believers are called to show.
Key language: Greek agape, biblical love, charity.

## Who Wrote Romans
Romans was written by Paul, the apostle Paul, to believers in Rome.

## Historical Context Of Ephesians
Ephesians is associated with Paul's ministry connected to Ephesus. Historical context themes include unity in Christ, the church as Christ's body, and life in the Spirit.

## Sanctification
Sanctification is the process of being made holy by God's grace. Key language: sanctification, holiness, spiritual growth.

## Apocrypha Books
Apocrypha books (also called deuterocanonical books in some traditions) are writings included in some biblical canons and excluded in others.
Commonly discussed apocrypha books include Tobit, Judith, Wisdom of Solomon, Sirach, Baruch, and 1-2 Maccabees.

## Baptism Views
Common Christian baptism views include believer baptism (credobaptist), infant baptism (paedobaptist), and debates about immersion, pouring, or sprinkling.

## Romans 8:28 In KJV
Romans 8:28 (KJV): "And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose."

## Council Of Nicaea
The Council of Nicaea (also spelled Nicea in some user queries) was a major church history event in AD 325 and produced the Nicene Creed tradition.

## Sword Drill Study Plans
In the Sword Drill app, study plans organize guided reading and learning paths. Users can open study plans, follow daily or topical steps, and track progress.

## Resurrection In 1 Corinthians 15
1 Corinthians 15 explains resurrection, including Christ's resurrection and the future resurrection hope for believers.
