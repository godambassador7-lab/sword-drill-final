# SHARP Eval Targets

## Explain John 3:16
John 3:16 states that God so loved the world and gave His only Son so that whoever believes in Him should not perish but have everlasting life.

## Who Was Paul
Paul was an apostle of Jesus Christ, formerly known as Saul of Tarsus.

## Difference Between KJV And WEB
KJV means King James Version. WEB means World English Bible.
KJV and WEB are two Bible translations.

## Meaning Of Agape In Greek
Agape is a Greek term for self-giving love.

## What Is The Trinity
The Trinity is the Christian doctrine of one God in three Persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

## Who Wrote Romans
Romans was written by Paul the apostle.

## What Are Apocrypha Books
Apocrypha books are also called deuterocanonical books in some Christian traditions.

## Explain Resurrection In 1 Corinthians 15
1 Corinthians 15 teaches the resurrection of Jesus Christ and the future resurrection hope of believers.

## Where Is Romans 8 28 In KJV
Romans 8:28 in KJV speaks of all things working together for good to those who love God.
