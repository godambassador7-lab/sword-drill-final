## What Is a Biblical Canon?

**Canon** comes from the Greek *kanōn* (κανών), meaning *rule, measuring rod, standard*.

A biblical canon is:
- A **recognized collection of sacred writings**
- Considered **authoritative Scripture** by a faith community

### Key Clarifications
- Canon ≠ inspiration itself
- Canon = recognition, not creation
- Communities recognized canons over time
