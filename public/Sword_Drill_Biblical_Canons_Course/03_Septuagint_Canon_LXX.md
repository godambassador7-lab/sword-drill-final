## Septuagint (LXX) Canon

### Origin
- Greek translation begun ~3rd century BC in Alexandria

### Includes
- All Hebrew books
- Additional Greek Jewish writings

### Common Additions
- Tobit
- Judith
- Wisdom of Solomon
- Sirach (Ecclesiasticus)
- Baruch
- 1–4 Maccabees
- Additions to Daniel & Esther

### Importance
- OT of early Christians
- Quoted extensively in NT
- Preferred by Church Fathers
