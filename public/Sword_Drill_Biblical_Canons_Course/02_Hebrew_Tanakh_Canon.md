## The Hebrew (Jewish) Canon – Tanakh

### Structure (TaNaKh)
- **Torah (Law)** – 5 books
- **Nevi'im (Prophets)** – Former & Latter
- **Ketuvim (Writings)** – Psalms, Wisdom, History

### Total Books
- 24 books (same content as 39 Protestant OT, different grouping)

### Characteristics
- Hebrew language (some Aramaic)
- Closed canon by ~1st century AD
- No Apocrypha

### Authority Basis
- Prophetic authorship
- Temple usage
- Covenant continuity
