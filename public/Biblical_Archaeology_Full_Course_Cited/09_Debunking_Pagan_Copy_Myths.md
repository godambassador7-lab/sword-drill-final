# Debunking Pagan Copy Myths (Horus, Mithras)

## Horus (Egyptian Religion)
Relief collections: British Museum
Image: https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/galleries/egyptian-sculpture
Scholarly Source:
- Erik Hornung, *Conceptions of God in Ancient Egypt*

Claims of virgin birth or resurrection are absent from primary Egyptian texts.

## Mithras (Roman Mystery Cult)
Tauroctony relief
Repository: British Museum
Image: https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/G_1899-1014-1
Scholarly Source:
- Roger Beck, *The Religion of the Mithras Cult in the Roman Empire*

Mithraism postdates Christianity and lacks historical resurrection claims.
