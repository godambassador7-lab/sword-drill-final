# The Dead Sea Scrolls (DSS)

## Discovery & Location
Found in caves near Qumran (Judea), 1947–1956.
Site Authority: Israel Antiquities Authority
Image: https://www.imj.org.il/en/exhibitions/dead-sea-scrolls/qumran-caves

## Great Isaiah Scroll (1QIsaᵃ)
Repository: Israel Museum, Shrine of the Book
Image: https://www.imj.org.il/en/collections/1989-700
Source: Eugene Ulrich, *The Dead Sea Scrolls and the Origins of the Bible*.

## Scroll Jars
Repository: British Museum
Image: https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/W_1986-0301-1
Source: Geza Vermes, *The Complete Dead Sea Scrolls in English*.

## Scholarly Significance
Confirms:
- Second Temple textual stability
- Judean provenance of Hebrew Scripture
- Local Jerusalem-centered theology
