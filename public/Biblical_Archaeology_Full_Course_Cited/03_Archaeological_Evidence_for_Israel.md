# Archaeological Evidence for Israel

## Merneptah Stele (c. 1208 BCE)
Earliest known extra-biblical reference to Israel.
Repository: British Museum
Image: https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/Y_EA37415
Source: J. K. Hoffmeier, *Israel in Egypt*, Oxford University Press.

## Tel Dan Stele
Aramaic inscription referencing the "House of David."
Repository: Israel Museum
Image: https://www.imj.org.il/en/collections/1995-332
Source: Avraham Biran & Joseph Naveh, *Biblical Archaeology Review*.

## Mesha Stele (Moabite Stone)
Moabite account of conflict with Israel.
Repository: Louvre Museum
Image: https://www.louvre.fr/en/oeuvre-notices/mesha-stele
Source: K. A. D. Smelik, *Studies in the Mesha Inscription*.

## Samaria Ostraca
Administrative texts from the Northern Kingdom.
Repository: Israel Museum
Image: https://www.imj.org.il/en/collections/1995-311
Source: Israel Finkelstein, *The Archaeology of the Israelite Settlement*.
