
# Sword Drill Associate-Level Course: Demonology in the New Testament

**Level:** Associate Degree (Survey + Exegesis + Theology)
**Credit Equivalent:** 6 credits
**Prerequisites:** Intro to NT, basic Koine Greek (recommended)

This course exhaustively documents and analyzes all New Testament accounts where Jesus Christ,
His apostles, or early believers encountered, confronted, or taught about demons / unclean spirits.
All material is text-driven with Greek analysis, theological synthesis, and assessments.

No fillers. No placeholders.
