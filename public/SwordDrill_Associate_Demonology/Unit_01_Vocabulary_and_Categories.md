
## Unit 1 — Vocabulary & Categories

### Greek Terminology
- δαιμόνιον — demon, malevolent spiritual being
- πνεῦμα ἀκάθαρτον — unclean spirit (Markan preference)
- ἐκβάλλω — to expel, drive out
- ἐπιτιμάω — to rebuke with authority
- σπαράσσω — to convulse violently (never used of Holy Spirit activity)
- ἐξουσία — authority, delegated power

### Biblical Categories
1. Demonization (indwelling control)
2. Affliction / oppression
3. Deception / false teaching
4. Temptation (testing)

Distinction between sickness and demonization is preserved by the text.
