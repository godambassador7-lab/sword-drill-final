
## Unit 2 — Demonology in Mark (Primary Dataset)

### Mark 1:21–28 — Capernaum Synagogue
Pattern:
1. Recognition of Jesus’ authority
2. Command to be silent
3. Violent convulsion (σπαράσσω)
4. Immediate expulsion

### Mark 3:20–30 — Beelzebul Controversy
- Satan divided cannot stand
- Blasphemy against the Holy Spirit defined as attributing God’s work to demons

### Mark 5:1–20 — Gerasene Demoniac
- Legion (many spirits)
- Self-harm, social isolation
- Restoration = clothed, sound mind, sent as witness

### Mark 9:14–29 — Epileptic-like Boy
- Disciples fail due to lack of prayer
- Jesus succeeds by authority alone
