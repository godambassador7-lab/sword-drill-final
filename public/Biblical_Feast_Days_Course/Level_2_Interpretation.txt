LEVEL 2 – INTERPRETATION
Feasts in Second Temple Judaism & the Life of Jesus

MODULE 1 – Second Temple Observance
The feasts were practiced with Temple-centered liturgy.
Pilgrimage feasts shaped national worship.
Calendar disputes existed but did not negate commandment.

MODULE 2 – Jesus and Passover
Jesus observed Passover within Torah context.
The Last Supper is covenantal, not a Roman invention.
Crucifixion timing aligns with Passover typology.

Matthew 26
John 19
Exodus 12

Resurrection language aligns with Firstfruits theology.
Pentecost marks covenant expansion, not replacement.

Water and light ceremonies contextualize Jesus’ teaching.
