LEVEL 1 – FOUNDATIONS
Biblical Feast Days: Origins & Commandments

This level establishes the biblical foundation of sacred time as instituted by God.
Leviticus 23 serves as the structural spine of this course.

MODULE 1 – Sacred Time in Scripture
God establishes time before institutions. The Sabbath precedes Sinai.
The Hebrew word 'moedim' refers to appointed times fixed by God, not human tradition.
Genesis presents time as structured and purposeful.

Key Texts:
Genesis 1–2
Leviticus 23:1–4 (LXX)
Psalm 103:19

MODULE 2 – Passover & Unleavened Bread
Passover marks redemption through substitutionary blood.
Unleavened Bread represents separation from Egypt and corruption.
These feasts are inseparable historically and theologically.

Key Texts:
Exodus 12–13
Leviticus 23:5–8

MODULE 3 – Firstfruits & Weeks
Firstfruits acknowledges God as the source of life and provision.
Weeks (Pentecost) completes the grain harvest and celebrates covenant faithfulness.

Key Texts:
Leviticus 23:9–22
Deuteronomy 16:9–12

MODULE 4 – Trumpets, Atonement, Tabernacles
Trumpets announce sacred assembly.
Atonement emphasizes mediation and cleansing.
Tabernacles commands joy and remembrance of provision.

Key Texts:
Leviticus 23:23–44
Numbers 29

ASSESSMENTS:
– Feast identification quizzes
– Sacred calendar ordering
– Vocabulary mastery
– Final exam
