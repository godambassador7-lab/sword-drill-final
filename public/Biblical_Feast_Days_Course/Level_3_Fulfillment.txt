LEVEL 3 – THEOLOGY & FULFILLMENT

MODULE 1 – Fulfillment Theology
Fulfillment does not mean erasure.
Shadows point forward; substance confirms reality.

MODULE 2 – Apostolic Teaching
Paul references feast language without mandating observance.
Hebrews presents priestly fulfillment.

Key Texts:
Hebrews 8–10
1 Corinthians 5
Acts 20:16

MODULE 3 – Eschatological Motifs
Trumpets signal resurrection language.
Tabernacles imagery appears in Revelation as God dwelling with man.

Key Texts:
Revelation 7, 21
Zechariah 14

MODULE 4 – Christian Practice
Observance is liberty, not obligation.
Feasts are instructional, not salvific.
