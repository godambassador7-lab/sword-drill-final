
Sword Drill Associate-Level Course: Biblical Archaeology

Level: Associate (Certificate Program)
Credit Equivalent: 3 credits

Scope:
A comprehensive study of the archaeology of the biblical world,
focusing on material culture, inscriptions, geography, and historical context
from the Bronze Age through the Roman period.

Method:
Archaeological, historical, and textual integration.
Scripture and material evidence examined together.
Non-denominational.
No fillers. No placeholders.
